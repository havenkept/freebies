Common questions

Is my project data saved?

Yes, in your own browser on your own device. Nothing is uploaded and nobody else can see it. That also means clearing your browser data clears your projects, so use "Save my work" if you want a copy you keep.

Why only three projects?

This is the Projects page from StashKeeper, our paid planner, capped so you can try the real thing rather than a mockup. The full version has no limit, plus your yarn stash, tools, gift planning and ideas.

Can I move my projects into the full planner?

Yes. Click "Save my work" and you'll get a file. In StashKeeper, use Import and pick that file. Your projects, row counts and progress come across exactly as they are.

Does the row counter work on my phone?

Yes. The buttons are sized for thumbs, and because everything runs in the browser you can keep it open beside you while you work. It keeps counting with the screen off and the page still open.

Do I need an account?

No. No account, no email, no download. Open the page and start counting.
