Common questions

How do I make a wedding seating chart?

Start with your confirmed guest list, then decide table sizes — most round tables seat 8 to 10. Seat the couple and wedding party first, then family, then friends in groups who already know each other. Leave the awkward pairings until last, when you can see what space is left.

How many people fit at a wedding table?

A 60-inch round seats 8 comfortably and 10 at a squeeze. A 72-inch round seats 10 to 12. Long banquet tables run about one guest per 24 inches of edge. Always seat fewer than the maximum if there will be chargers or big centrepieces.

Do I need assigned seats or just assigned tables?

Assigned tables are enough for most weddings and far less work. Full place cards only really pay off for a plated dinner with meal choices, where servers need to know who ordered what.

When should the seating chart be finished?

About two weeks before, once RSVPs are in — but expect to redo it. Late cancellations are guaranteed, which is exactly why a chart you can change in seconds beats one drawn on card.
