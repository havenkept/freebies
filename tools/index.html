Why these are free

Each one does a single job properly. If it turns out you want the whole thing, the full planners are on Etsy, and your work from these tools carries across rather than being retyped.

Common questions

Do I need an account?

No. Nothing here asks you to sign up, and nothing you enter leaves your device.

Will my work still be here tomorrow?

Yes, as long as you come back in the same browser on the same device and have not cleared your browsing data. Several tools also let you download a copy.

Do these work on a phone?

Yes. They are built to work on a phone first, though the seating chart is easier on a bigger screen since you are dragging things around.

Can I print them?

The trackers print cleanly, with the buttons and banners stripped out.
